Mobimo --- Agent & Product Context

Product

Mobimo is a WhatsApp-based AI companion that helps Nigerian women
understand their physical health, with a flagship focus on postpartum
recovery. It provides education, tracking, AI-guided educational
answers, warning-sign alerts, healthcare navigation, reminders, and an
on-demand personal health report.

Primary users

New mothers in the first weeks to months postpartum.

Especially women with limited access to follow-up care or postnatal
education.

Priority users include underserved, low-resource communities
reachable through WhatsApp.

Secondary users

Healthcare professionals and community health workers who may benefit
from organized recovery summaries.

MVP

Chat-based postpartum recovery tracker.

General health and symptom tracker.

Educational AI health assistant.

Postpartum warning-sign alerts.

Menu-driven education hub.

Rest reminders.

Personal PDF health report.

Basic healthcare resource directory and postnatal check-up
reminders.

Product principles

WhatsApp first: no separate app is required for the MVP.

Simple: use clear language, numbered menus, and natural
conversation.

Educational: Mobimo informs users but does not diagnose.

Safety first: postpartum red flags are checked before ordinary
responses.

Minimal data: collect only what is needed and obtain clear consent.

Action oriented: when a user may need care, make the next step
clear.

Out of scope for MVP

Payments.

Human clinician handoff.

Wearable integration.

Full multi-language NLP.

Separate companion app.

Success areas

Reach.

Knowledge improvement.

Consistent tracking and engagement.

Healthcare-resource access.

Postnatal check-up attendance.
